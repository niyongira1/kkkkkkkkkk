A GIS and Python Based Optimization Model for Rural Low Voltage Network Design

Musanze District, Rwanda, supporting universal electrification. Interactive companion outputs to the written dissertation.

Proposed Network Additions, Simulation

Beyond the dissertation's own scope, this model proposes where the network still needs work and simulates it directly on the map: transformer sites needing a rated capacity, MV line extensions to reach them, and LV poles required on steep terrain to keep the conductor at REG's minimum ground clearance. All three are on by default when you open the map below.

Honest note: this run's 23 proposed sites are all real, already-surveyed transformer locations that simply lacked capacity data, not new greenfield sites, so their proposed MV tie-in length came out to 0 km. The moment a transformer crosses 100 percent loading, this same pipeline will site and connect a genuinely new transformer automatically, snapped to the nearest road and checked against a 1.2 km transformer-to-customer and 1.3 km road-to-customer maintenance-access limit.

Interactive Web Map

District, sector, cell, and village administrative boundaries, roads, existing MV lines and transformers, unelectrified households, and all four network variants, conventional, MST, STP, and combined terrain plus electrical, with toggleable layers and clickable transformer popups showing real capacity and loading. Five base map choices are available from the layer control: streets, satellite imagery, terrain, light, and dark. Also includes Musanze's urban core (Secondary City), its 2 rurban centers, and 74 nucleated rural settlement sites, sourced from Rwanda's national settlement hierarchy dataset, so the rural target area is visible against the urban boundary it is defined relative to.

Shown by default:

  • Red dots: all 3,863 real unelectrified households, the demand this whole model exists to serve
  • Tan shaded area: Musanze town's urban core and rurban centers, for context; zero of the 3,863 households fall inside it
  • Blue plus markers: proposed new transformer sites, popup shows source transformer, capacity, and households served
  • Dashed gold lines: proposed MV line extensions, popup shows length and estimated CAPEX
  • Orange dots: proposed poles inserted for terrain vertical clearance
